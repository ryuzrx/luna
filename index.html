Mac

Download the app

  1. Click Download for Mac
  2. Open the zip, drag Luna to Applications
  3. First launch: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway
Uses your Claude or ChatGPT plan if you're signed in to them.

Just open it

  1. Open Luna in any modern browser
  2. Chrome / Edge: click Install in the address bar to keep it
